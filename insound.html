Insound.

Bands, musicians, artists, whatever we call ourselves... Insound was built for you. The people behind Insound have been through the same financial burdens of having to pay for equipment, travel, rehearsal rooms, recording, mixing, mastering, distribution, and even some pay-to-play gigs.

With an average of ~0.02p for each stream, it's incredibly difficult to make any sort of return on your music, especially if you're new and unsigned.

Insound are dedicated to being free, forever. We take 20% from each sale to cover running costs such as development, storage, servers, security, coffee, etc.
